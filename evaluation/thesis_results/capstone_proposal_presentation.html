Problem / Opportunity

Scope and Limitations

⏱ 40 sec

✅ Scope

  • Counts ornamental fish automatically
  • Tracks fish variants and inventory records
  • Provides web-based monitoring and reports
  • Uses IoT sensors/camera and database integration
  • Accessible only to authorized users

🚫 Limitations

  • Limited to ornamental fish only
  • May not detect overlapping or very small fish
  • Requires stable internet and power supply
  • Tested only in the selected client environment
  • Does not include sales forecasting or financial analysis
Speaker Notes: The system covers automated counting, variant tracking, and web monitoring. Key limitations include dependency on stable connectivity and testing restricted to the client's environment.
